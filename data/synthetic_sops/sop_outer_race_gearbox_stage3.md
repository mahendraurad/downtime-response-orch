## Purpose
Outer race fault on bottleneck gearbox assets. Do not begin repair without the spare bearing on site.

## Prerequisites
CRITICAL STOP: Do NOT open gearbox without PART_003 physically on site.
PART_003 standard lead time 30 days, expedited 12 days.

## Safety and LOTO
Enforce Zone D access control and LOTO_004 procedures.

## Tools and Parts
PART_003 SKF22318-E, gearbox tooling, support stands.

## Step by Step Procedure
1. Confirm PART_003 is on site.
2. Secure gearbox and isolate power.
3. Remove housing covers per safety procedure.
4. Inspect outer race before replacement.
5. Install new SKF22318-E bearing and torque per spec.

## Part Shortage Protocol
Derate 15-20% and monitor every 2h while waiting for expedited procurement.
Pre-book the repair window once the spare is available.

## Lessons from Cases
Zero safety stock on critical spare is unacceptable for bottleneck assets.
