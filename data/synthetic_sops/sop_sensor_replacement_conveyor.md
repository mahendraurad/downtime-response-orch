## Purpose
Replace or secure sensor connections for CH_31B on conveyor assemblies where burst dropout patterns appear.

## Prerequisites
CRITICAL WARNING: LOTO_003 expired 2026-04-15. Must be renewed before any work begins.

## Safety and LOTO
Confirm renewed permit LOTO_003 and verify lockout of conveyor power.

## Tools and Parts
Calibrated torque screwdriver, replacement terminal connectors, cleaning supplies.

## Step by Step
1. Verify expired LOTO_003 has been renewed.
2. Isolate the conveyor sensor circuit.
3. Inspect the J-301 terminal and cable connection.
4. Re-terminate the connector and torque to 5-6 Nm.
5. Restore power and verify signal quality.

## Critical Specifications
Terminal torque: 5-6 Nm using calibrated torque screwdriver, NOT by hand.

## Lessons from Cases
Expired LOTO permits cause delay and are a hard stop for sensor fault work.
